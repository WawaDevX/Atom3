# Contributing to Atom3

Hello! Thanks for your interest in contributing. :D

## Can I contribute?

Of course! Anyone is welcome to contribute as long as you follow one golden rule:

* **Test your code.** (fully!)

---

## AI & LLM Policy

We understand that AI tools can speed up development, but unvetted AI contributions often make codebases messy and hard to maintain.:( 

If you want to use an LLM (like Chatgpt, claude, or copilot) to assist with your code, you must follow these rules:

1. No more than 30% of your total pull request code can be generated by AI.
2. You must fully understand every line of code generated by the model.
3. You must rewrite all AI generated comments in your own words.
4. You must write your PR title and description. Do not use AI to generate them.
5. Test your code thoroughly before submitting.

> **Warning:** Breaking any of these rules will result in an immediate rejection of your Pull Request, and may lead to a block from the repository.
